🗓 Overview & Timeline

This structured plan takes you from zero to newspaper-reading proficiency. Consistent daily study of 30–60 minutes is the key. The plan follows the official HSK (汉语水平考试) framework.

Level-by-Level Milestones
MilestoneYou can...
HSK 1 CompleteRead basic signs, menus, simple messages. Recognise 150 characters.
HSK 2 CompleteRead simple social media posts and short conversations. 300 characters.
HSK 3 CompleteRead simple stories, travel guides, and everyday notices. 600 chars.
HSK 4 CompleteRead most informal text, basic news headlines. 1,200 characters.
HSK 5 CompleteRead newspapers with occasional dictionary lookup. 2,500 characters.
HSK 6 CompleteRead any newspaper, novel, or professional text fluently. 5,000+ chars.
